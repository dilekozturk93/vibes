Stage 3 — SCC analysis of the pre-balance pair-graph

Pre-balance pair-graph has 4 strongly-connected components, not 1. This is the structural reason for the 3 __balance__ edges EulerianBalancer adds: SCCs must be bridged before an Euler cycle can exist.

Each colour in the diagram is one SCC. Visually inspect which pair-vertices are stranded together — the structural disconnects are immediately obvious.

Why are there 4 SCCs? PairGraphTransformer filters out __end__ transitions (line 122–124 of PairGraphTransformer.java) — so pair-vertices whose only "escape" is via __end__ land in their own SCC. INIT is its own SCC (in-degree 0 by construction). The earlier text diagnostic only showed ONE of the resulting disconnects (state25 group cannot reach state6); the SVG below shows there are several similar islands.

Stage 4 — Balanced pair-graph (after EulerianBalancer)

Edge types: grey = original real pair-edge, blue = __dup__ edge (Chinese Postman doubling of a real pair-edge along the BFS shortest path), orange dashed = __balance__ edge targeting INIT, thick red dashed = __balance__ edge targeting a NON-INIT vertex.

Counts: 130 real + 159 __dup__ + 3 __balance__. Of the __balance__ edges, 2 target INIT (structurally necessary because pair-graph INIT has in-degree 0) and 1 targets a non-INIT vertex (the rare case caused by SCC disconnect under __end__ filtering).

Diagnostic conclusion

Structural cause, not a bug. The pair-graph construction is consistent with its design (synthetic __end__ transitions are filtered out so the coverage metric counts only real action pairs). On product configurations where a state's only escape is via __end__, the pair-graph naturally fragments into multiple SCCs. EulerianBalancer's Chinese-Postman BFS handles within-SCC imbalance via __dup__ doublings; the between-SCC bridging falls through to __balance__ because no real path exists across the disconnect.
What this means for the fix plan: Defect B (split at every synthetic edge in splitAtSyntheticEdges, including __dup__) is the primary defect — fixing it makes the suite split at only 3 points instead of 162, and on this product 2/3 of the resulting segment-starts land at INIT (executable from the FTS initial state). The remaining non-INIT __balance__ (1 in this product) leaves 1 segment that begins mid-FTS — to be documented in Threats to Validity. Defect A (pair-graph construction's INIT in-degree 0) is deferrable to post-draft because it would require a coverage-semantic change.
